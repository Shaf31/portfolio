About Me

I'm Elshafey Ahmed. On paper, I was supposed to be an engineer. But in 2016 I started teaching myself design after hours — and what began as a side obsession became the whole plan by 2020, when I shifted into design full-time. The engineer in me never left though: it shows up in how I structure a layout, solve a brief, and sweat the details nobody notices but everybody feels. Now, as a Senior Graphic Designer specializing in visuals, I design, direct, animate, and retouch — making every frame a little better than it needs to be.
